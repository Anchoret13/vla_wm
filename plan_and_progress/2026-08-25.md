## But the comparison that judges it cannot support the judgement

Three measurements, in order of how much they matter.

### 1. A zero-information rule wins the bar, by luck

Constant-index baselines on the fresh 8-anchor test bank — no model, no state,
no actions:

| rule | top-1 regret | best at |
|---|---:|---:|
| always pick candidate 4 | **0.000930** | 5/8 |
| always pick candidate 0 (= the reference) | 0.003125 | 3/8 |
| always pick candidate 1 | 0.007842 | 0/8 |
| **M0.3 residual** | 0.008557 | — |
| always pick candidate 2 | 0.010508 | 0/8 |
| always pick candidate 3 | 0.010406 | 0/8 |

"Always pick candidate 4" beats every model and every registered baseline. It
knows nothing.

On the **48 dev-train anchors** the same rule is the **worst** of the five
(regret 0.0233) and is argmax at 3/48, while candidate 0 is argmax at 28/48:

```text
argmax index counts over 48 dev anchors: {0: 28, 1: 10, 2: 5, 3: 2, 4: 3}
mean regret: pick0 0.01115  pick1 0.01008  pick2 0.01158  pick3 0.01480  pick4 0.02334
```

So the test bank's pattern is a small-sample fluke that inverts on 6× the data.
**Top-1 regret over 8 anchors cannot rank these methods**: the ordering it
produces is dominated by which candidate index happened to win.

### 2. The correlation sign does not replicate either

M0.2 scored **−0.2275** on 2M.4's held-out split and **+0.1719** on this fresh
one. Same checkpoint, same metric, two disjoint 8-anchor banks, opposite signs.
Both estimates rest on 32 candidate points.

That retroactively weakens a claim I made on 2026-08-24 — that M0.2's ranking
was "anti-correlated, not merely uninformative." On a second bank it is
positively correlated. The honest statement is that **the correlation is not
identified at this sample size**, in either direction.

### 3. The physical channel is exactly degenerate

Across all 8 test anchors, the maximum absolute true physical delta between any
candidate and its reference is **0.000e+00** — bitwise identical
`post_prefix_delta` for all five candidates at every anchor. 2M.4 saw this at
43/48 anchors; here it is 8/8.

So `L_physical`, at weight 0.5 — a third of the registered objective — is
regressing a constant zero. M0.3's near-perfect 0.000011 on that channel is
trivially correct and carries no information, and M0.2's 0.000627 is worse than
predicting nothing.
