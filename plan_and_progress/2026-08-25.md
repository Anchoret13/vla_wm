## Mainline interpretation

The residual formulation is worth retaining. It uses the action difference
(shuffle gap **+0.0206**), gives `Δ_hat(reference)=0` exactly, and produced a
positive effect correlation (**+0.1643**) on this split. But it was trained from
only **48 independent anchors** with a 32,505-parameter head. That is the fact to
act on: the model needs substantially more independent state-action training
examples.

Two secondary observations affect the next training run without becoming
separate projects:

- the 8-anchor comparison is too small for a final selector decision, so the
  next training bank includes a larger held-out split rather than opening an
  evaluation-only action;
- the physical-effect target is constant at almost all of these anchors, so it
  is removed from the next loss and training is concentrated on the continuous
  phase-consequence target.

This does **not** send the project back to proposal, anchor, horizon, noise-floor,
or evaluation diagnostics. It sends it directly to a larger WM training run.
