## Why chain2b

The 08-26 triage closed two of three candidate testbeds. `chain1b@250` fails on
30–60-step trajectory-time deficits and `chain3@750` sits behind a capability
wall (stock 0/12, atomic 1/12) — neither is a *decision* error, so no selector
can help. `chain2b@500` is the one testbed where the failures are decisions:

| first object | success | rate |
|---|---:|---:|
| cream | 97/104 | 0.933 |
| tomato | 0/18 | **0.000** |

Pooled over three panels. The correct behaviour is already inside policy support
(~84% unprompted), so this is a selection problem, not a capability problem.

`pi_0` frozen 64-seed baseline: **51/64 = 0.797**, CP95 [0.697, 0.875]. Ten
convertible failures (8 tomato-first, 2 none) put the ceiling at 61/64 = 0.953.

## The commitment window (v096)

The first correction attempt (v094, 10-action atomic-cream prefixes) redirected
**0/4** targets. A teacher-length sweep found the threshold:

| teacher length | redirected | succeeded |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 10 | 0/4 | 0/4 |
| **40** | **4/4** | **4/4** |

4/5 controls preserved. So `c = 10` is a constraint on *WM credit assignment*,
not on how long an executed correction must be. Everything downstream uses a
40-step commitment window.

## Route A — DAgger distillation (v097)

Atomic prompt as a **training-time** mechanism only (framework §6.4); deployment
stays full-prompt `N=1`. Two passes: collect teacher action sequences under the
atomic-cream prompt, then replay open-loop and distil at full-prompt boundaries.
Acquisition seeds 4900–4989, disjoint from the 3200–3399 panel.

```
30/30 teacher rollouts reached the cream pick; 2,150 steps
trainable: action_out_proj 32,800
epoch 0 fm=0.31418 / 1 fm=0.11393 / 2 fm=0.24588  over 215 pairs
|head delta| 8.960e-04; 8,600 env steps
```

| | success | cream-first | tomato-first |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| `pi_0` | 51/64 = 0.797 | 54 | 8 |
| `pi_1` | 52/64 = 0.812 | 58 | **5** |

Paired: gained 3, lost 2, **McNemar p = 1.000**.

**Verdict: mechanism confirmed, magnitude insufficient.** Tomato-first fell 8→5
and cream-first rose 54→58, so the distillation moves the first-object prior the
intended way — but a 8.96e-4 head delta after 3 noisy epochs does not move
success. Not a null result; an under-trained one.

## Route B — the deploy-time WM (v098/v099)

Weights unchanged, deployment prompt unchanged. The WM answers an
action-conditioned outcome question — *does this candidate chunk commit to the
success mode?* — and the argmax is executed at each of the first 4 boundaries.

**`QΦ` is deliberately not the target, and this is a finding.** The phase
potential scores progress on the first *incomplete* goal atom, and the BDDL lists
tomato first — so a `QΦ`-maximising selector rewards the move that fails 0/18. It
is the §3.1.1 ordered-prefix defect resurfacing in the reward rather than in the
progress measure. Four action items were built on `QΦ` as the selection target;
on this task it is worse than useless.

**Training cost: zero environment steps.** At each acquisition state, chunks
sampled under an atomic-cream prompt are positives and under an atomic-tomato
prompt are negatives. The labels are not guesses — they are the measured
93%/0% split above. The state is always encoded under the **full** prompt,
because that is the only prompt available at deployment.

```
dataset 320 chunks over 40 states, 160 positive
state-disjoint split: train 240 / val 80
held-out (state-disjoint) accuracy 1.000 at epoch 39; train 0.979
0 environment steps
```

Held-out accuracy 1.000 on a *state-disjoint* split: the WM separates
cream-directed from tomato-directed chunks from `(full-prompt state, action)`
alone. `--n 1` reproduces stock behaviour exactly and is the control.
