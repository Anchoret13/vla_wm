## Decision

Retain the project identity and final endpoint:

> world-model learning must transform the pretrained VLA state into a
> language-conditioned predictive state, use that state to generate a
> policy-improvement signal, and improve recurrent full-prompt `N=1` pi0.5 on
> public LIBERO LoHo.

V7.1 combines two borrowed mechanisms without becoming an implementation of
either paper:

- **MPAIL-inspired policy improvement:** exchangeable canonical full-prompt
  pi0.5 samples represent the action prior, and task-conditioned model scores
  produce a prior-regularized finite-candidate teacher. Atomic/distinct-goal
  proposals instead form an explicitly registered exploration mixture; they do
  not justify a canonical pi0.5-prior claim. Both are distilled by flow matching
  into recurrent full-prompt `N=1` pi0.5.
- **MPAIL2-inspired learning loop:** real learner transitions accumulate in
  one off-policy replay union; predictive state, transition outcomes, tail
  value, and policy are trained as separate modules and updated from that
  replay.

The scientific object remains the LC predictive state. Do not adopt MPAIL's
deployment-time MPPI, MPAIL2's task-agnostic latent as the main model, or an
adversarial observation-only reward. We already have action-labeled
demonstrations, GoalSpecs, executed branch outcomes, and language task
identifiers. Planning/reranking is allowed only as a training-time teacher and
data-acquisition mechanism. Final deployment remains recurrent full-prompt
`N=1` pi0.5.

```text
accumulated real transition replay
                 ↓
 language-conditioned predictive state + transition
                 ↓
 task outcome model + distributional tail value
                 ↓
 pi0.5 proposals → prior-regularized model teacher
                 ↓
 weighted flow post-training of pi0.5 action expert
                 ↓
 recurrent full-prompt N=1 LoHo behavior
                 ↓
 train-split stalls/recoveries append to the replay union
```

- [ ] Once the direct outcome head has nontrivial held-out ranking signal,

  `Q_ell^{pi0}(z_t,u_t)` ensemble from accumulated multi-horizon continuations.

## V7.1.4 — MPAIL-inspired pi0.5 flow post-training

At a replay state, sample a finite policy-supported candidate set
`C={u_i}`. Let `S_LCB(z_t^ell,u_i)` be the frozen model's lower-confidence
outcome/tail score. The training-time improvement distribution is

\[
w_i=
\frac{m_i\exp(S_{\mathrm{LCB}}(z_t^\ell,u_i)/\beta)}
     {\sum_jm_j\exp(S_{\mathrm{LCB}}(z_t^\ell,u_j)/\beta)}.
\]

For exchangeable canonical pi0.5 samples, sampling represents the prior and
`m_i` is uniform. Expanded exploration requires a separately frozen family
mixture; it is not an exact canonical pi0.5-prior update.

- [ ] Begin with a learned hard-LCB teacher until held-out score ordering is
  calibrated. Add ordinal-soft weights only after the learned hard selector
  beats stock and a score-permuted selector on executed held-out anchors.
- [ ] Distill with the normalized weighted sum of **per-candidate** flow losses
  under shared flow noise/time. Never flow-match a weighted-average action
  chunk.
- [ ] Credit only the physically executed first ten actions. Replace the
  unexecuted target suffix by the registered branch-independent constant before
  the bidirectional action suffix enters the loss graph.
- [ ] Fine-tune only the policy boundary frozen by V7.1.0B. The recurrent LC
  state must enter that boundary; no learned score, GoalSpec, or privileged
  simulator value is available at deployment.
- [ ] Preserve full-prompt conditioning, demo rehearsal, state-local stock
  trust, equal hierarchical state mass, common train schedules, and exact
  pi0.5 initialization across policy arms.

The minimal policy-training comparison is:

| arm | data / PEFT / rehearsal | model weighting |
|---|---|---|
| stock `N=1` | frozen pi0.5 | none |
| BC-only PEFT | matched V7.1 budget | uniform/no LCWM improvement weights |
| LCWM-weighted PEFT | matched V7.1 budget | LC outcome/tail LCB |

Score permutation is an anchor-gate control, not a fourth full LoHo training
run. Same-checkpoint recurrent/reset, shuffled-language, and
task-agnostic/readout-only attribution are required only after the main arm
improves executed behavior.

- [ ] Freeze training steps, checkpoint cadence, environment-evaluation
  cadence, and checkpoint-selection rule before launch.
- [ ] Save videos for every scheduled simulator evaluation at every evaluated
  checkpoint under the run/video contract.
- [ ] Select by registered hierarchical behavior metrics, never by offline flow
  loss or visual inspection.

### Post-run objective audit — what V7.1.1 actually supports

The audit unit is a unique state/action transition, with source as the
independent generalization unit. Candidate rows, fidelity repeats, GoalSpec
relabels, and continuation repeats are not independent samples.

- Raw replay is `770 = 564 train + 206 dev` rows over `40 = 31 train + 9
  dev` anchors and `19 = 14 train + 5 dev` source episodes. The split is
  source-disjoint.
- Exact `(anchor, actions_env bytes)` deduplication leaves `724 = 533 train +
  191 dev` unique transitions. The 46 audit-only duplicates are the 40
  `u0_rep2` fidelity executions plus six duplicated stored-dev-winner actions.
- All sibling branches at each of the 40 anchors share the same initial
  external/wrist image. This is valid crossed-action structure for physical
  transition learning.
- At a 2 mm sibling-relative threshold, only 31 train rows over seven anchors
  and six sources have task-object effects; dev contains zero such rows. After
  removing scripted servo, 25 rows over four anchors/four sources remain and
  cover only T1/T3/T5. Generic robot/visual dynamics are abundant, but current
  dev cannot measure held-out task-object physical generalization.
- The crossed table contains 2,221 `(transition, goal)` rows, but has zero
  `0→1` flips, zero `1→0` flips, zero ordered-prefix deltas, and zero
  `q_valid` deltas over the executed ten actions. Static task states can differ
  across goals, but no action×language semantic transition effect is present;
  copying a task code is sufficient.
- No same-goal paraphrase or `text_variant_id` rows were materialized. The
  desired paraphrase invariance is therefore not trainable from this replay as
  written.
- Only 175/770 rows have `R=2` continuation summaries (`166 train + 9 dev`),
  and no raw continuation observations/actions were stored. Across 350 outcomes
  there are zero successes and zero damage events. These rows are canonical
  `Q^{pi0}` summary targets, not multi-step dynamics replay.
- In r2, the 152 candidate-versus-`u_0` comparisons are `5 win / 142 tie / 5
  loss`. All five wins are expanded-prompt branches from one T2 source and two
  anchors; all 98 canonical full-prompt comparisons tie. This is insufficient
  multi-task support for a learned policy teacher.

The correct status is therefore:

> V7.1.1 is a useful physical sibling-action bank and a valid reason to start
> model training. It does not yet provide the semantic transition supervision
> needed to claim language-conditioned dynamics, learned outcome ranking, or
> policy improvement.
