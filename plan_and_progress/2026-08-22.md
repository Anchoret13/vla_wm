## Decision basis

Action 2M.1 completed `B_boot-v2` and trained M0.1. The widened bank produced
two-sided held-out ranking support, and the action path is measurably used, but
M0.1 is not useful as a selector:

- matched prediction loss is `0.816777` versus `0.696529` for state-only;
- non-tie ranking accuracy is `3/48 = 0.0625`;
- train contains only `21/512` non-tie pairs;
- validation contains `0/128` non-tie pairs, so checkpoint selection cannot
  observe the ranking head;
- two of the three registered conditions pass, while
  `prediction_loss_gain > 0` fails.

Per framework §16.3, this is a second state-only result. The next scientific
change is therefore the **candidate proposal family**, not another benchmark,
horizon, anchor, or architecture diagnostic.

**Objective:** replace the current “more iid PI0 samples + FPS/random” candidate

PI0 support, then train one M0.2 and determine whether it learns useful
action-conditioned prediction and sibling ranking.

### Locked controls

Action 2M.2 changes one scientific component only: the proposal family. It
keeps fixed:

- the `chain1b_lr2@250` mechanism setting and stock full-prompt PI0 policy;
- `tau=160`, executed prefix `c=10`, and continuation outcome clock starting at
  `tau+c` with `H=80`;
- the bootstrap tensor schema and source-level split ownership;
- the current M0 architecture, state-only capacity-matched baseline, physical
  and outcome heads, non-tie-balanced ranking loss, optimizer, and fixed
  30-epoch schedule;
- validation checkpoint selection followed by one opening of a sealed test
  split.

No Action-2P calibration row, prior opened test row, smoke row, or aborted row
may enter the new train, calibration, or test splits.

### Proposal-family change

1. Draw raw candidate chunks from stock PI0 exactly as before; do not optimize
   arbitrary actions outside policy support.
2. Before any branch outcome exists, map each executed first-10 chunk to fixed
   action-only effect proxies: end-effector translation direction/magnitude,
   rotation magnitude, and gripper-close timing.
3. Select a fixed, mutually exclusive quota across those effect strata. This
   replaces FPS/random selection; simply increasing the number of iid samples
   or siblings is not the intervention.
4. Freeze the proxy definitions, stratum quotas, candidate count, RNG mapping,
   source queues, and interaction cap in the executable manifest before the
   first environment step. Branch outcomes never participate in proposal
   generation or candidate selection.

### Data collection and split rule

- Collect fresh, source-disjoint development and test banks under permanent
  `model_train`, `model_calib`, and `selector_assess` ownership.
- Keep the previous scale as the minimum: **32 train, 8 calibration, and 8
  fresh test anchor groups**. Test sources and their candidate schedules are
  sealed before development outcomes are used.
- Use a prospectively fixed, deterministic label-stratified development split
  so both train and calibration contain `better` and `worse` non-tie pairs.
  The exact minimum support and source cap must be frozen before collection;
  groups and repeats remain nested and are never counted as independent source
  episodes.
- If the fixed development cap cannot supply two-sided train and calibration
  support, stop the proposal-family action without training M0.2. Do not repair
  the deficit by moving opened test groups, changing thresholds, or starting a
  benchmark diagnostic.

### Training and readout

After the development bank and calibration split are sealed:

1. train the action-conditioned M0.2 and identically initialized state-only
   baseline once under the locked 30-epoch schedule;
2. select each checkpoint using only the ranking-aware calibration split;
3. open the fresh test split once;
4. report matched prediction loss, physical MSE, binary-outcome Brier score,
   rank macro accuracy, absolute non-tie accuracy, and action-shuffle gap.

### Required deliverables

- proposal-family implementation and immutable execution manifest;
- fresh `B_boot-v3/groups.pt` plus source/split/interaction ledgers;
- `M0.2/best.pt` containing action-conditioned and state-only checkpoints;
- complete `metrics.jsonl` training curves;
- one `summary.json` with the held-out comparison and absolute ranking support;
- an execution-day result entry that reports all environment interaction,
  including technical or aborted attempts.

### Completion and handoff

Action 2M.2 is complete when the registered collection/training attempt and all
required artifacts are closed, regardless of sign. M0.2 is eligible for the
next acquisition experiment only if all prospectively registered usefulness
conditions hold together:

1. `prediction_loss_gain_vs_state_only > 0`;
2. `rank_macro_accuracy_gain_vs_state_only > 0`;
3. `action_model_shuffle_gap > 0`;
4. absolute held-out non-tie accuracy exceeds the registered chance baseline.

If these hold, proceed directly to the matched-budget WM-versus-diversity-versus-
random acquisition experiment. Only an acquisition win can authorize a policy
update. If they do not hold, M0.2 is not used as a selector; the result triggers
a formulation decision rather than another benchmark/horizon/anchor diagnostic
or a sweep of the same architecture.
