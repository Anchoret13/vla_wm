Three findings, in order of importance:

1. **Positives are real, and one is a conversion.** At `a3493`, sibling `rand1`
   beat its matched reference on all three CRN keys and turned `failure@250`
   into `success@250` on **3/3** keys where the reference succeeded **0/3**. At
   `a3494`, three siblings are paired-positive; there the gains are in
   progress/timing, not conversion — no sibling there reached success.
2. **The binding failure is the absence of negatives, not of positives.**
   Exactly **one** paired-non-improving sibling exists across all 8 anchors, so
   condition 6 scores 0/8. The pilot was designed to require both, because a
   world model needs ranking signal in both directions; what it found is
   one-sided.
3. **Five of eight anchors are completely inert** — every one of 18 comparisons
   is an exact tie at `H=80`.

Differences accumulate with horizon, and the sealed floors are doing real work:
6/8 anchors differ in raw readouts while only 3/8 survive the quantization
floors. Reporting the raw 6/8 as "variation" would have passed condition 4 and
turned this HALT into an ADVANCE. The floors were derived from the automaton's
10-step evaluation stride and sealed before execution, which is the only reason
that call was not available to make after the fact.

### A design defect the data exposed, for the next revision

`a3480`'s **reference succeeds on 2 of 3 CRN keys.** Its source was a genuine
`failure@250`, so it passed the registered eligibility rule — but that source
failed under *its own* continuation noise, and at the same anchor the stock
reference usually succeeds. So the anchor is not a failure anchor under the
paired protocol at all, and its single "non-improving" sibling is a sibling that
merely succeeded less often than a mostly-succeeding reference.

Eligibility was defined by the **source's** outcome; the paired comparison is
against a **freshly drawn reference**. Those are different objects. A future
stratum should require the reference itself to fail on all (or a registered
majority of) CRN keys before the anchor is admitted.
