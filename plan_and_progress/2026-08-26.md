# 2026-08-26 — the WM was never the bottleneck; three testbeds triaged

Execution record for Action 2M.6 (registered in [`2026-08-25.md`](2026-08-25.md))
and for the free exploration authorized after it. **150,452 environment steps.**

The headline is a correction to the project's working assumption. Four action
items — 2M.2 through 2M.5 — treated the world model as the thing to fix. It was
not. An oracle selector produces **no policy improvement at all** on the task
those items were run on, and the reason is a property of the task rather than of
any model.

## 1. Action 2M.6, first two steps

| artifact | result |
|---|---|
| `M_0` bootstrap on all 72 groups | trained; **validation selected epoch 1 of 60** |
| `pi_0` frozen panel, `chain1b@250` | **14/32 = 0.438**, CP95 [0.287, 0.597] |

`M_0`'s calibration loss rises monotonically (0.245 → 0.405) while training loss
falls 8× (0.250 → 0.030). On 64 anchors the 32k-parameter residual head
memorizes immediately, so `M_0` is effectively untrained.

`pi_0`'s successes cluster hard against the deadline: **13 of 14 land in the
last 20 steps** (218–249, median 241).

## 2. Pre-spend measurements that redirected the action

Before spending round 0's 61,310 steps, three things were measured from existing
data:

- **`QΦ` is the right target.** corr(`QΦ`, success@250) = **+0.2300** over 1,680
  branch rows; mean `QΦ` 0.638 for successes vs 0.506 for failures. A *perfect*
  `QΦ` predictor reaches the oracle ceiling exactly. The 2M.4 readout change was
  correct.
- **Success-flip ceiling: 8/72 = 11.1%** of anchors have any alternative that
  beats the reference on success rate.
- **Verified-correction bar: 61/288 = 21.2%** for a random alternative; oracle
  50%.

## 3. The oracle policy update — the decisive negative

36 verified corrections already existed in the 72 executed anchors at **zero new
acquisition cost**. Fine-tuning the framework's locked narrow boundary
(`action_out_proj` only, zero LC bias) on them and re-running the frozen panel:

| | successes | CP95 | mean milestones |
|---|---|---|---|
| `pi_0` | 14/32 = 0.438 | [0.287, 0.597] | 1.38 |
| `pi_oracle` | **14/32 = 0.438** | [0.287, 0.597] | 1.44 |

Paired on the same seeds: **gained 4, lost 4, McNemar p = 1.000**, completion
1.2 steps *slower*. The fine-tune itself worked — correction FM loss 0.181 →
0.066 with retention held at 0.070 — so this is not a training failure.

**Even perfect selection does not improve the policy.** The world model was
never the bottleneck.

## 4. Root cause: the anchor was in the wrong phase — and moving it does not help

`pi_0`'s failures are **16/18 already-picked-cannot-place**. The registered mask
requires `ever_achieved = {}` at `tau=160`, so **every anchor ever collected in
V8 is pre-pick**. Three action items collected corrections in a phase where the
failure does not occur.

That looked like the whole story. It is not:

| anchor placement | oracle flip ceiling |
|---|---|
| pre-pick, `tau=160` | 8/72 = 11.1% |
| **post-pick, `tau≈213`** | **1/10 = 10.0%** |

At the phase-aligned anchor the reference succeeds **0.00 at all 10 anchors**.
With 20–30 steps left, no 10-action prefix recovers a 30–60 step deficit.

`[CLOSED]` **`chain1b@250` cannot demonstrate the method**, on three independent
tests: 11.1% pre-pick ceiling, 10.0% post-pick ceiling, null oracle update. Its
failures are **trajectory-time deficits, not decision errors.**

## 5. Testbed triage

| task | `pi_0` | failure type | local-flip ceiling | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `chain1b@250` | 0.438 | **temporal** — needs 30–60 more steps | 11.1% / 10.0% | closed |
| `chain3@750` | **0.031** | **capability wall** | stock 0/12, atomic 1/12 | closed |
| **`chain2b@500`** | **0.812** | **decision error** | — | **qualifies** |

### `chain3@750` — decisional in form, capability wall in substance

`pi_0` = 1/32; **30/32 stop at exactly 4/6 milestones**; median last progress
step 260 of 750; **median idle tail 490 steps = 65% of the episode.** The policy
has two thirds of the episode left and will not start the third object.

At 12 stall anchors (`tau=300`, `H=200`), reaching `pick_up cream_cheese_1`:

```text
reference                      0.00
stock alternatives             0/12
ATOMIC-prompt prefix           1/12
atomic prefix + atomic cont    0/12
```

V7.7's encouraging 3/8 came from a **reacher-built** state with the arm
pre-positioned. From the state π0.5 reaches on its own after two placements, the
behaviour is absent. Privileged prompting does not recover it.

### `chain2b@500` — the first setting that qualifies

The failure is **order-determined**, and it replicates across two independent
panels:

| panel | cream-first | tomato-first |
|---|---|---|
| V8.0 confirmation (n=30) | 20/22 = 0.909 | **0/6 = 0.000** |
| frozen panel (n=32) | 26/28 = 0.929 | **0/4 = 0.000** |
| **pooled** | **46/50 = 0.920** | **0/10 = 0.000** |

Four properties hold simultaneously for the first time in this project:

1. genuine failures — 0/10, not clock-truncated;
2. a real decision point — which object to approach, early in the episode;
3. a **common cause** — going for tomato first;
4. **the correct behaviour is already inside policy support** — π0.5 chooses
   cream-first unprompted in ~80% of episodes.

Property 4 is what `chain3` lacked and what 36 state-specific `chain1b` prefixes
could never have. The correction re-weights a choice the policy already makes
correctly most of the time, so it has a mechanism for transferring across states.

## 6. Power, before spending anything further

Only 4 of 32 frozen-panel seeds are tomato-first, so a **perfect** fix yields
30/32 at McNemar **p = 0.125** — unresolvable.

| panel | expected tomato-first | p if all converted |
|---|---:|---:|
| 32 | 4 | 0.1250 |
| **64** | **8** | **0.0078** |
| 80 | 10 | 0.0020 |

This is framework §19's rule — a panel must be sized to the effect it resolves —
recurring at the behavioural layer. The reserved 3200–3399 block exists for
exactly this. A 64-seed `chain2b` baseline is the next measurement.
