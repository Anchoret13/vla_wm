## 3. Does the deployed latent retain the task state, or only the clock?

`scripts/diag_v253_event_probe.py`, zero environment steps. CLAUDE.md names this class
of diagnostic as the one that "would have explained four to five candidate failures"
and was run only afterwards. It had still never been run on the representation this
round's whole construction reads from.

For each of chain3's three goal-predicate bits, a linear probe on episode-disjoint
folds, comparing three feature sets: the boundary index alone (`clock`), the 8217-d
rich latent alone (`latent`), and both. Exact ridge over a **whole lambda path** from
one eigendecomposition — the first run used a single fixed lambda and the ordering
reversed when the sample was doubled, which is what a fixed penalty does when n
changes, not what a representation does. No lambda is selected; the path is reported.

Pooled D0 + D1, 14,400 boundaries, 96 + 96 episodes, five folds, AUC:

| bit | object | base rate | clock | latent | latent+clock | latent − clock |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | alphabet soup | 0.788 | 0.949 | **0.975–0.981** | 0.975–0.981 | +0.026 … +0.033, 5/5 folds at every lambda |
| 1 | tomato sauce | 0.633 | 0.972 | 0.975–0.982 | 0.976–0.982 | +0.003 … +0.010, 2/5 folds below lambda=100 |
| 2 | **cream cheese** | 0.016 | 0.848 | **0.757–0.871** | 0.824–0.886 | **−0.091 … +0.023**, negative at every lambda ≤ 1000 |

**The two objects chain3 places are linearly decodable from the latent at ~0.98 AUC.
The third — the one the task fails on, the one the whole residual is gated to
rescue — is decodable at 0.76–0.87, and does not beat a clock until the heaviest
regularisation in the path.** The combination beats the clock from lambda ≥ 10, so the
latent is not devoid of information about that object; it is weak where every head in
the round has to read.

That is a bound on the construction, shared by `M1`, `M0` and `Q` alike, and it is a
candidate explanation for the deployed head's 0.389 decision-region correlation
against 0.844 over all boundaries.

**What would overturn it.** A linear probe is a lower bound: a nonlinear head may
recover the bit, and a null here is evidence about linear decodability only. Bit 2's
base rate is 1.6% — on D0 alone two of five folds were single-class and the effect was
larger (−0.192 at lambda 0.1); D1 supplies the positives that make five folds scorable.
Both samples agree for lambda ≥ 1. Artifacts:
`results/v253_event_probe/chain3_lr2_d0_2026-09-16T212316Z/` and
`chain3_lr2_d0d1_2026-09-16T212408Z/`.

### What these three sections say together

Read as a chain, on data that existed before the 09-13 panel was ever opened:

1. the representation encodes the object the task fails on at 0.76–0.87 AUC against a
   clock's 0.848, while encoding the two objects it does place at ~0.98 (section 3);
2. the head read off it has no decision-region signal on D0 and a fivefold split range on
   the deployed bundle, with the deployed instance at the top of it (section 10);
3. the transition is genuinely better than its controls at predicting latents, and is
   nevertheless worse than a constant at the ten-chunk horizon the interface rolls;
4. so reading Phi' off the rolled latent in the decision region is no better than not
   rolling.

An advantage built from (2) on top of (3) in the region described by (1) has nothing to
carry, which is what the deployment result reported: `M1` vs `M0` p = 1.0000, `M1` vs
`M1_shuf` p = 1.0000, and the no-rollout control `Q` at or above `M1` on every endpoint.

**This does not say a latent world model cannot help a VLA, and it does not retire
chain3.** It says this object, at this horizon, read through this head, in this region,
carries nothing — and every one of those four is a design choice that was never measured
before 10.6 h of GPU time was spent on it. All four measurements cost zero environment
steps and all four were possible on 2026-09-12.

**What would overturn it:** a shorter roll (H = 3 is inside the useful range on this

### Two corrections the verification of section 8 forced

**`d_min_final_atom` is not null, and the sentence above covers only the tested
endpoints.** On the fourth registered secondary endpoint — estimation-only, carrying no
registered test — M1 approaches 0.0204 m closer to the final object than `M1_shuf`,
Wilcoxon p = 0.0415 uncorrected, bootstrap CI [−0.0047, +0.0463] spanning zero. It is
the one nominal effect in the whole secondary set that favours the rolled-forward model
over its action-shuffled ablation. It is also the endpoint the pre-registration
disqualifies from being read as the outcome, because on the calibration ladder it moved
*opposite* to the outcome: twice the magnitude approached better and grasped worse. So:
the registered-test evidence is null for the roll, and the single nominal effect for it
sits on the endpoint the contract says not to read that way. Both halves belong in the
record.

**`phi_basin_mean` and `d_min_final_atom` condition on a post-treatment variable.** Both
are defined only on episodes with `final_atom_boundaries > 0`, and the number of
episodes that collapse differs by arm — Q/M0/M0_cont 9, base 10, M1_shuf 12, M1 13,
rand 14 of 288 — while the collapsed episodes have mean stage 1.2–1.6 against 4.0–4.3
for the retained ones. Every contrast on those two endpoints therefore discards an
unequal share of each arm's worst episodes, and for `phi_basin_mean:M1-vs-base` it
discards 13 of M1's against 10 of base's, biasing that row *in M1's favour* — a row
reported above as a Holm rejection favouring M1. It cannot be imputed, because the
endpoint is undefined where it is missing. It can only be stated, which is what this is.
