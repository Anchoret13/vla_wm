## What V7.4 is

One complete mainline vertical slice — data acquisition, LCWM
training, teacher ledgers, policy fine-tuning, fresh public-LoHo
behavior — that repairs the three audited V7.3 defects as registered
objective/interface corrections, NOT as sweeps:

1. **Interface**: the pooled common-mode handoff is replaced by ONE
   registered centered residual, with ONE registered fallback
   (learned-query token conditioning) selected by a frozen mechanical
   decision rule. No other interface variant may be trained.
2. **Supervision**: model-teacher eligibility requires nontrivial
   registered advantage over `u0`; new acquisition targets the named
   missing support cells across all five tasks.
3. **Exposure**: the policy schedule fixes task -> source -> anchor
   before arm identity; abstention anchors remain as zero-model-loss
   cells; no anchor may absorb the whole model budget.

Phase-1 criterion unchanged: a trained LCWM-conditioned π0.5 that
beats stock π0.5 on task-balanced terminal success on the fresh
development panel, under recurrent full-prompt `N=1` deployment.
Nothing terminates at an offline metric.

## V7.4A — interface layer and content assertions

**Registered interface (primary).** For pooled state
`p = Pool(z_t^ℓ) = z.mean(dim=1)`:

```
c_t = (p − μ_train) / σ_train
```

with `μ_train` the uniform mean over the COMPLETE V7.4 policy-state
set (all real policy-anchor pools — grounded, model-eligible, and
abstention — plus all demo-rehearsal pools) and `σ_train` the scalar
population RMS of the centered pools over that same set. Both are
computed once from the frozen V7.4C checkpoint, saved in the run
lineage, and never recomputed after the first policy forward. The
flow boundary is unchanged: `v' = v + W_c c_t`, `W_c` bias-free
zero-init, trained jointly with stock-initialized `action_out_proj`;
everything else frozen. The constant-state control arm uses `c = 0`
(the centered analogue of the V7.3 global-mean arm).

**Registered fallback (only alternative).** One learned-query block:
a single trainable query attends over the LCWM's 4 state tokens
(single-head scaled dot-product, d=384), and the attended vector
replaces `p` in the same centering/normalization and the same
`W_c` boundary. No planner, no best-of-N, no deployment change.

**Frozen non-collapse thresholds** (dimensionless, σ_train = 1
units, computed over the complete anchor set, not one probe; reset
and order-only-shuffled variants are built per anchor with the V7.3D
order-only construction):

- T1 identity: median pairwise ‖c_i − c_j‖ over distinct real
  anchors ≥ 0.30;
- T2 history: ‖c_real − c_reset‖ ≥ 0.05 for ≥ 2/3 of anchors with
  decision index ≥ 5;
- T3 order: ‖c_real − c_shuffled‖ ≥ 0.05 for ≥ 1/3 of anchors with
  decision index ≥ 5;
- T4 paraphrase: median same-goal paraphrase distance ≤ 0.5 × the T1
  median (crossed-text anchors only);
- T5 goal separation: median compatible-alt-goal distance ≥ 1.5 ×
  median paraphrase distance (crossed-goal anchors only).

**Frozen decision rule.** The probe instrument runs twice:

1. **A-stage diagnostic** on the existing V7.3C LCWM checkpoint and
   V7.3 anchor set (instrument validation; the 2026-08-07 audit
   numbers are prior data and this run is report-only). If the
   centered POOLED interface fails T1 or T2 here, V7.4 commits to the
   fallback interface immediately — the pooled candidate is not
   retrained on V7.4C states.
2. **Binding gate before V7.4D** on the frozen V7.4C checkpoint over
   the complete V7.4 policy-state set. Failing the binding gate on
   the committed interface triggers the one registered fallback
   switch (if not already taken) and one re-run of the gate. A second
   failure halts V7.4 before policy training with status
   "state-content deficiency localized to the LCWM"; that is a
   result about V7.4C's state, not a license for an interface sweep.

**Training-time checks.** At policy step 50: over all scheduled
anchors, `Var(W_c c) / (‖mean(W_c c)‖² + Var(W_c c)) ≥ 0.5`
(V7.3's measured value was ≈ 1e-8). Failure halts that policy run
once, switches to the fallback if untaken, and restarts; otherwise
halts V7.4D. Final-step post-projection variation is recorded and
named in interpretation but does not gate V7.4E: behavior remains
the endpoint.

## V7.4D — teacher, matched schedule, policy fine-tuning

**Teacher eligibility (registered change).** A model-selected
candidate becomes a generated target ONLY if, under the frozen dev
margins, its predicted registered outcome tuple strictly dominates
the SAME anchor's predicted `u0` tuple with margin ≥ the frozen dev
margin on ≥ 1 component and ≥ 0 on all. Identity selection
(winner = `u0`'s candidate) and predicted ties are abstention.
Matched-random permutes candidate identity within the same eligible
cells. Ledgers sealed outcome-blind before any policy step; unique
eligible anchors reported by task, source, and phase.

**Matched schedule (registered change).** Built by task -> source ->
anchor round-robin (the `lcwm/v073_schedule.py::rr_schedule`
construction) over the FULL teacher-anchor set — eligible AND
abstention — plus the grounded set. All four arms consume the
identical anchor sequence. At abstention anchors the model/random
term is computed with weight exactly zero (present, not skipped).
Grounded, model, and random streams receive equal scheduled mass per
task; a single anchor's visit count may not exceed
`ceil(stream_length / n_anchors_in_its_task_stream)`. The schedule
tensor and its per-anchor visit histogram are saved and asserted
before step 0.

**Arms** (unchanged set): stock (untrained), correction_bc
(constant `c = 0`), lc_grounded, lc_full, lc_random. Trainable
parameters, optimizer (300 steps, AdamW 1e-4/1e-4, clip 1.0,
checkpoint every 50, final-step rule), demo rehearsal, suffix/
retention trust, and the A/B/C + reset/shuffled readouts are
inherited from V7.3D, except readouts now aggregate over the full
anchor set (median + per-anchor rows) instead of one probe.
Training saves per-step scheduled-anchor ids and per-checkpoint
`W_c c` statistics for the exposure and content audits.
