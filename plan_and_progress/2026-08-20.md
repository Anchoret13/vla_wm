## 1. Objective checkpoint

The project objective is unchanged:

> Can a pretrained imitation-learned VLA improve from its own deployment failures by using targeted counterfactual interaction to refine a decision-relevant world model and distill better actions back into the policy?

The evidential sequence remains:

1. failure-anchored branches add action-effect and outcome support;
2. a learned WM acquires useful branches more efficiently than matched random/diversity selection;
3. WM-guided targets improve the final full-prompt `N=1` VLA beyond direct imitation of the same verified corrections;
4. a second bounded round moves the failure frontier and improves behavior beyond a matched-total one-shot update.

The benchmark band, task count, episode deadline, anchor rule, and value estimator are experimental instruments. They matter because they can invalidate a measurement, but they are not the scientific objective. V8.0 and Action 1R executed no sibling-action coverage experiment, no world model, and no policy update. Their outcomes therefore provide **zero direct evidence for or against all four method claims**.

Do not substitute `terminal`, `irreducible`, `stall_onset`, or `earliest_unrecoverable` without the corresponding evidence.

## 4. Action 2P contract

### Question

At distinct, stratum-matched pre-failure histories from stock rollouts that miss `L=250`, do VLA-supported first-10-action siblings produce repeatable local action-effect/outcome variation, including both improvements and non-improving outcomes under the same continuation noise?

### Non-claims

Action 2P does not test:

- world-model prediction or model-guided acquisition;
- policy improvement;
- two-task or LoHo generalization;
- continual learning;
- unlimited-horizon competence or terminal-failure recovery.

It does not update a model or policy, and its rows are permanently excluded from later training and behavior evaluation.

### Frozen source and anchor

- Task/policy: `chain1b_lr2`, stock `pi0.5`, full prompt, deployment `N=1`.
- Horizon: `L=250`, a post-calibration experimental finite-horizon objective frozen before this pilot; it is not an intrinsic-capability horizon or external deployment requirement.
- Fresh source seeds: all `3480–3499`; run all 20, never early-stop at quota.
- Failure: valid non-success at step 250. A technical-invalid source triggers `TECHNICAL_HALT`, remains charged, and is not replaced or counted as a normal non-failure. Report both deadline failures `F/20` and exact-mask-eligible failures `E/20`.
- Anchor: fixed `tau=160`, because `160 + c=10 + H=80 = 250`.
- Literal anchor kind: `retrospective_deadline_backoff`.
- Exact eligible mask at `tau`: achieved/current-valid/damaged empty; pick actionable; pick and place unresolved.
- Selection: after the complete source panel closes, seal the first eight eligible failures in seed order. Underfill HALTs; failed replay is not replaced with the ninth source.

The source suffix establishes `failure@250` only. It is conditioned to fail and therefore cannot be used as a reference outcome.

### Candidate and execution design

For each of eight independent anchors:

1. after all sources and anchors are sealed, define reference `u0` as the first draw from a registered hash-derived stock-`pi0` RNG key, with no action-geometry or outcome selection and outside the `N=32` alternative draws; never use the source's cached post-`tau` action as reference;
2. draw exactly `N=32` raw stochastic `pi0` alternatives from separate presealed RNG keys;
3. deduplicate on the executed first `c=10` actions and require at least 16 unique alternatives;
4. select three farthest-point-diversity candidates using a preregistered standardized action feature/distance/start/tie-break, then select three disjoint matched-random candidates from the remaining pool;
5. globally hash-seal all eight pools, chunks, selections, execution order, and CRN schedules before any branch outcome is visible;
6. execute the reference and all six alternatives under the same three presealed continuation RNG keys;
7. after the ten-action prefix, replan with stock `pi0` and read outcomes after `H={20,40,80}` continuation steps; the ADVANCE decision uses `H=80` at deadline 250, while `H=20/40` and prefix effect are diagnostics only; never step officially beyond 250.

Each alternative therefore has three paired reference comparisons. Repeats are nested within an anchor; the independent scientific denominator is eight anchors, not 168 branches.

No proposal feature, pool selection, or tie-break may use the source trajectory after `tau`, its terminal mask/failure phase, or any branch outcome. The existing safe lexicographic contract supplies outcome components, priority, safety, and replay-noise floors; Action 2P replaces its best-of-three aggregation with same-key pairing. At `H=80`, a `paired-positive` must beat its matched reference on all three RNG keys. A `paired-non-improving` alternative never beats reference and is strictly worse on at least one paired key beyond replay noise. The exact deadline-outcome vector, directions, ties, and noise floors are sealed before execution. Reference variability and prefix physical effect by themselves do not count as candidate-induced deadline-outcome variation.

```text
branches: 8 × (3 ref + 6 × 3 alt) × 90   = 15,120
hard N_env cap                           = 20,120
```

Every started or aborted environment segment is charged. There are no retries, substitute anchors, extra assessment branches, post-hoc quota increases, or budget reallocations. The ledger uses distinct `source`, `ref_prefix/ref_cont`, `div_prefix/div_cont`, and `rand_prefix/rand_cont` cap lines with source/anchor/candidate/CRN/policy hashes, actual official steps, and termination. Its worst case is 356 segments: 20 sources plus 168 prefixes and 168 continuations. A pre-segment headroom check prevents another execute-then-overrun failure. Candidate proposal count and GPU-seconds are reported separately.

### ADVANCE gate — not V8.1 PASS

All must hold:

1. all 20 sources close; every eligible failure is reported and exactly the first eight in the presealed order are selected; reserved-seed use is zero;
2. the same checkpoint and mask hash are restored for all 168/168 branch starts; all 24/24 reference prefixes meet the registered numeric replay tolerance;
3. every pool retains at least 16 unique first-10-action alternatives and executes the sealed paired schedule within the cap;
4. at primary `H=80`, at least 4/8 anchors show candidate-induced registered deadline-outcome variation;
5. at primary `H=80`, at least 2/8 anchors contain a paired-positive sibling; and
6. at primary `H=80`, at least two anchors each contain both a paired-positive and a paired-non-improving sibling.

Primary gates are reported per anchor at `H=80`; `H=20/40` are reported alongside them as secondary diagnostics. `diversity` versus `random` yield may be described, but the pilot is not powered or designed as a selector-efficiency comparison.

### HALT interpretation

HALT if any ADVANCE condition is unmet, including source/stratum underfill, provenance/replay failure, pool underfill, cap overrun, post-deadline official stepping, or failure of the joint positive/non-improving gate.

An outcome HALT means that this anchor/proposal/setting did not expose sufficient counterfactual support. It does not justify training or sweeping a world model. The next change must be one bounded anchor or proposal/setting revision, not a lower bar, a larger post-outcome budget, or indefinite benchmark shopping.
