### The ceiling, measured before trying a fifth candidate

Applying U0's discipline to §4: train a probe **directly on the target**, supervised
and episode-disjoint. If that cannot order failures, no reward objective can.

| task | supervised ceiling ρ |
|---|---:|
| chain1b | **+0.617** ± 0.060 |
| chain2b | +0.414 ± 0.203 |
| **chain3 (target)** | **+0.201** ± 0.080 |

So on `chain3` the bar of 0.35 sits **above the achievable ceiling**. Every candidate
was being asked to clear a height nothing can reach.

Four follow-ups, all negative:

| variation | chain3 ceiling |
|---|---:|
| readout: terminal / mean / endpoints / maxmin | 0.201 / 0.185 / 0.120 / 0.125 |
| rich latent (per-camera mean+max, 8217-d) | 0.222 terminal, 0.261 mean |
| learning curve n = 60 / 94 / 140 / 191 | 0.163 / 0.083 / 0.236 / 0.214 — **flat** |
| graded progress (stage + earliness) | **0.298** |

Not the readout point, not the pooling, not the sample size. **The best chain3
ceiling reached is 0.298.**

### What actually limits it

`chain3` failures are **homogeneous**: 182 of 191 stop at exactly stage 4. "Order
failures by progress" has almost nothing to order, so any rank statistic is near
zero by construction. My earlier reading — "the latent lacks progress information" —
over-attributed this to the representation. The graded measure (stage plus how early
the last milestone landed) raises the ceiling from 0.214 to 0.298 precisely because
it restores resolution among those 182.

### §6.1's bar was task-independent; the ceiling is not

A bar above the ceiling is **unfalsifiable** — no method can pass and the test loses
all discriminating power. The fix is to express it relative to what is achievable:

```
was:  ρ ≥ 0.35                       a constant from the survey
now:  ρ ≥ 0.6 × the task's supervised ceiling
```

**Self-check, because changing a LOCKED criterion after seeing results is exactly
where self-deception lives: does this rescue any candidate? No.**

| task | new bar | prior value | R1 | R4 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| chain1b | 0.37 | 0.247 ✗ | 0.095 ✗ | −0.131 ✗ |
| chain3 | 0.18 | — | 0.041 ✗ | −0.110 ✗ |

The change makes the test **stricter** on the easy task (0.35 → 0.37) and *possible*
on the hard one. It flips no verdict.

### Where this leaves §4

R1, R2, R3, R4 dead. R5 (a VLM as reward model) is now the targeted next try rather
