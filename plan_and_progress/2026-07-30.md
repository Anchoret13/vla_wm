# 2026-07-30 — LC-Flow v0.6: instantiate the model we actually mean

**Original status: ACTIVE ACTION QUEUE. Current status: FROZEN iteration-1

## Decision

The H7 public-LoHo result is a valid negative result for the implemented
v0.5 teacher/readout/adapter contract:

| arm | SR | Q |
|---|---:|---:|
| stock | 0.040 | 0.516 |
| v0.4 current-WM legacy | 0.040 | 0.512 |
| v0.5 reset random | 0.040 | 0.472 |
| v0.5 reset WM | 0.000 | 0.482 |
| v0.5 recurrent random | 0.040 | 0.470 |
| v0.5 recurrent WM | 0.000 | 0.070 |

It is not a clean negative test of language-conditioned predictive dynamics.
The executed implementation violated the intended contract in four coupled
ways:

1. only `3/20` train and `4/20` dev snapshot groups contained
   continuation-Q sibling variation;
2. continuation randomness was not paired across siblings, and the
   100-action endpoint usually washed out the first-ten action effect;
3. candidate actions transitioned a language-independent physical prior,
   while current task features remained a readout bypass;
4. the policy targets were generated from reset state and the recurrent
   policy gate remained effectively shut.

Therefore the next step is not to tune this implementation. It is to build,
train, and behaviorally evaluate the model represented by the actual
hypothesis:

```text
crossed visual-action-language training data
                    ↓
language-conditioned recurrent predictive state
                    ↓
action-conditioned prediction in that same state
                    ↓
grounded branch preference / policy targets
                    ↓
π0.5 flow-interface post-training
                    ↓
better full-prompt N=1 public-LoHo behavior
```

Offline model diagnostics are recorded, but they are no longer go/no-go
decisions in front of policy training. Only a mechanically invalid job—data
leakage, an absent action path, incorrect continuation pairing, failed reload,
or a broken stock-parity contract—may stop the fixed v0.6 run.

## Non-negotiable v0.6 implementation graph

Let \(h_t^\ell\) be the real-prompt π0.5 visual-language feature and let
\(u_{t-c:t}\) be the executed action history. The deployed state is one
task-conditioned recurrent predictive state:

\[
\begin{aligned}
\bar z_t^\ell
  &=T_\theta\!\left(z_{t-c}^\ell,E_a(u_{t-c:t})\right),\\
z_t^\ell
  &=U_\theta\!\left(\bar z_t^\ell,h_t^\ell\right).
\end{aligned}
\]

`U` may use a fixed-bound observation-anchored residual for stability, but
the result is still one state. For candidate \(u_i\):

\[
\widetilde z_{t+c}^{\ell,i}
=T_\theta\!\left(z_t^\ell,E_a(u_i)\right).
\]

Current-state grounding and candidate prediction are two disjoint decoder
calls:

\[
\begin{aligned}
\widehat y_{\mathrm{current}}^\ell
  &=D_{\mathrm{current}}(z_t^\ell),\\
\widehat y_{\mathrm{phys}}^i
  &=D_{\mathrm{next,phys}}(\widetilde z_{t+c}^{\ell,i}),\\
\widehat y_{\mathrm{task}}^{\ell,i},
\widehat r^{\ell,i},
\widehat V^{\ell,i},
\widehat p_{\mathrm{success}}^{\ell,i},
\widehat s^{\ell,i}
  &=D_{\mathrm{next,task}}(\widetilde z_{t+c}^{\ell,i}).
\end{aligned}
\]

\(D_{\mathrm{current}}\) may predict only current automaton/history grounding.
Every candidate-dependent next/effect/reward/value/ranking output must decode
from \(\widetilde z_{t+c}^{\ell,i}\).

The real next observation may correct the prior only after the predicted
outputs have been formed:

\[
z_{t+c}^\ell
=U_\theta\!\left(
  \widetilde z_{t+c}^{\ell,\mathrm{executed}},
  h_{t+c}^\ell
\right).
\]

The same \(z_t^\ell\), not a separately constructed policy state, conditions
π0.5:

\[
b_t=\eta\tanh\!\left(W_z\operatorname{Pool}(z_t^\ell)\right),
\qquad W_z=0\ \text{at initialization},
\]

where \(\eta\) is fixed. This is added to the action-expert conditioning. It
has exact stock parity at initialization and a direct gradient to \(W_z\);
there is no separately learned scalar gate that can remain closed.

### Required language behavior

For the same physical history and candidate action:

- same-goal paraphrases should produce equivalent predictive states and
  outcomes;
- compatible distinct goals may produce different task-semantic predictive
  states and different reward/progress/value predictions;
- physical outcome prediction must remain invariant to the goal relabel.

This is the project's hypothesis. A language-conditioned readout over a
language-independent candidate transition is a baseline, not the v0.6 method.

### Forbidden shortcuts

Candidate heads may not consume:

- current \(h_t^\ell\), current anchor, or current \(z_t^\ell\) alongside the
  transitioned state;
- \(D_{\mathrm{current}}\) features, logits, or predictions;
- the true next observation or next posterior;
- raw `task_id`, active-subgoal ID, phase ID, or a second language embedding;
- a direct candidate-action bypass that avoids \(T_\theta\);
- prompt-bound features copied from the physical transition's original
  instruction when the sample is relabeled.

Privileged simulator state may select and label training samples. It is never
a model or policy input.

### Candidate and replay contract

At each snapshot, construct:

```text
u_support                  recorded source/support chunk; outside policy pool
policy_candidates[0]       designated full-prompt π0.5 reference sample
policy_candidates[1:4]     three exchangeable full-prompt π0.5 alternatives
u_replay                   exact repeat of policy_candidates[0]
```

For the 40 accepted groups this yields 200 unique first-ten branches plus 40
replay-noise repeats. `R=2` continuations over the five unique candidates
yield at most 400 records per goal-conditioned continuation policy. Rejected
bounded-search attempts add first-ten/replay records but do not receive
expensive continuations unless they are explicitly retained as null
calibration.

- [ ] Execute only the first ten actions of each chunk from the same restored
  snapshot.
- [ ] Use the repeated candidate to estimate restore/replay noise for every
  group.
- [ ] Mark a group `effect_resolved` only when a measured physical or
  predicate difference exceeds repeated-action noise. Action-space \(L_2\)
  does not qualify it.
- [ ] Mark a group `policy_rankable` only when
  `policy_candidates[0:4]` have a non-tied task outcome under paired
  continuations.
- [ ] Mark a group `support_only` when `u_support` is useful but
  the full-prompt pool remains tied. Keep all three group types and route them
  to the appropriate losses.
- [ ] Never place `u_support` in the WM/random teacher pool or include it in
  the \(3/4\) exchangeable-argmax calculation.

### Paired continuation contract

- [ ] From `u_support` and every `policy_candidates[0:4]` branch outcome, run

- [ ] Run continuations separately for the canonical prompt of every
  registered compatible GoalSpec. A continuation collected under goal
  \(\ell_a\) may not become a \(V_{\ell_b}^{\pi_0}\) label by semantic
  relabeling.
- [ ] Same-goal paraphrases share the canonical representative policy/value
  label for their GoalSpec. If a distinct GoalSpec has no own continuation,
  mask its continuation-value/success-return loss and use that crossed row
  only for immediate physical/task-semantic supervision.
- [ ] For a fixed
  `(snapshot_id, goal_spec_id, continuation_repeat, continuation_decision)`,
  use the identical flow-noise stream for every sibling candidate.
- [ ] Save and assert continuation seed and noise hashes, policy checkpoint,
  GoalSpec/prompt hash, action normalization, and horizon.
- [ ] Expose outcomes at 10, 30, 60, and 100 continuation actions rather than
  using only the washed-out endpoint.
- [ ] If a competent/demo continuation is also run, store it as
  `G_support`. Never mix it with or call it \(G^{\pi_0}\).

### Task automaton and outcome labels

Replace monotone "ever completed" Q with an explicit task automaton:

- event milestones such as grasp, lift, open, and close;
- currently valid placement and terminal predicates;
- ordered-prefix state;
- \(0\!\rightarrow\!1\) and \(1\!\rightarrow\!0\) predicate flips;
- contact, attachment, object and fixture pose;
- invalidation/damage and recovery;
- reward, terminal success, and valid Q at every registered horizon.

A grasp milestone may remain achieved after a correct release, while a placed
object knocked out of its target must invalidate placement. Immediate object
distance is stored only as a physical auxiliary; it is never an advantage.

For paired continuation \(r\), retain the registered outcome tuple

\[
Y_i^{(r)}
=
\left(
\mathbb 1[\mathrm{success\ by\ }100],
-D_{\mathrm{irreversible},i}^{(r)},
P_{\mathrm{valid},i}^{(r)}(100),
\frac{1}{4}\sum_{h\in\{10,30,60,100\}}Q_{\mathrm{valid},i}^{(r)}(h),
-\tau_{\mathrm{next},i}^{(r)}
\right),
\]

where \(P_{\mathrm{valid}}\) is the currently valid ordered-prefix fraction
and \(\tau_{\mathrm{next}}\) is time to the next automaton milestone, censored
to `101` if none occurs by the horizon. Compare these tuples
lexicographically in the written order and define

\[
A_{ij}
=
\frac{1}{R}\sum_r
\operatorname{pref}\!\left(Y_i^{(r)},Y_j^{(r)}\right),
\qquad
\operatorname{pref}\in\{-1,0,1\}.
\]

For each continuous outcome component, freeze the replay-noise tolerance as
the tranche-A train-source 95th percentile of exact-repeat disagreement. A
sibling pair receives \(A_{ij}=+1\) or \(-1\) only when both continuation
repeats agree in direction beyond those tolerances; otherwise it is a tie
\((A_{ij}=0)\). The model predicts the outcome components and their induced
pairwise preference; no arbitrary weighted sum or immediate geometry is
allowed to become the advantage.

### Crossed-language index

Every physical `(history, action, future)` is indexed under:

- the canonical composite instruction;
- at least two same-goal paraphrases;
- every distinct, scene-valid composite GoalSpec registered for that scene.

- [ ] Store raw observations and executed actions from episode reset through
  the branch outcome. For every text variant, recompute the entire
  prompt-conditioned prefix sequence and unroll both online and EMA recurrent
  states from episode reset; never initialize a relabeled state only at the
  snapshot.
- [ ] Re-evaluate immediate automaton, reward, progress, and success semantics
  under every GoalSpec. Attach continuation value/success-return only from a
  continuation actually run under that GoalSpec's canonical prompt.
- [ ] Store physical transitions once and semantic relabels separately.
- [ ] Do not use random scene-incompatible instructions as the principal
  negative.
- [ ] Atomic active-subgoal prompts may label auxiliary milestones but may
  not count as distinct composite tasks.
- [ ] If a scene has no valid distinct composite GoalSpec or no branch with
  task-semantic disagreement, its samples may train physics and paraphrase
  invariance but that scene family is excluded from the distinct-goal
  conditioning claim.

### History-contrast rows

Contact/late sampling alone does not force the model to use history. Build a
small explicit history-contrast index from real trajectories:

- [ ] Find source-disjoint pairs with current observations/privileged physical
  states matched within replay tolerance but different valid automaton
  histories—for example initially closed versus opened-then-closed, or never
  placed versus placed-then-invalidated.
- [ ] Collect at least two train pairs and one dev pair for every reversible
  task family supported by the public scenes; record unsupported families
  rather than manufacturing contradictory labels.
- [ ] Train `D_current(z_t^\ell)` on the true milestone/order/invalidation
  state from the full history and report recurrent-versus-reset history loss
  on these rows.
- [ ] Split pairs by source episode. Never attach two different history labels
  to the same historyless frame.

## V6.4 — Build model teachers without the 75% random-selection error

Freeze a source/phase-balanced manifest of approximately 200 train decisions.
It must include every source-train `policy_rankable` snapshot exactly; the
remaining states are sampled from the same registered contact/late/recovery
phase buckets. This prevents another teacher bank with zero overlap with
grounded candidate states.

At each new state generate one deterministic, exchangeable `N=4`
full-composite-prompt π0.5 candidate pool. At grounded snapshot states reuse
the executed `policy_candidates[0:4]` and their observed labels. Candidate
index 0 is always the designated exchangeable reference. These are
policy-supported candidates; support/oracle prompts do not enter this pool.

- [ ] For every teacher state, recompute the real-prompt feature sequence and
  unroll \(z_t^\ell\) from episode reset through the recorded observations and
  executed actions. Reset/cached-at-snapshot construction is forbidden.
- [ ] Use this same recurrent-state implementation and checkpoint in the
  scorer and deployed recurrent policy.
- [ ] Score every candidate only through
  \(z_t^\ell\rightarrow T(z_t^\ell,u_i)\rightarrow D(\widetilde z_i)\).
- [ ] Use the scalar \(s_i\) trained by the paired Bradley–Terry objective and
  define \(p_{i0}=\sigma(s_i-s_0)\). Do not reconstruct a hand-weighted
  candidate score from success/progress/damage heads.
- [ ] On source-disjoint non-tied dev pairs, freeze
  \(\delta=Q_{0.90}(|p_{ij}-\mathbb 1[A_{ij}=+1]|)\). If no non-tied dev pair
  exists, set \(\delta=0.5\), which emits no model teacher.
- [ ] Let \(i^\star=\arg\max_i s_i\), breaking exact ties by the saved
  candidate hash. Emit a non-stock teacher only when \(i^\star\ne0\) and its
  lower-bound preference over the reference exceeds chance:

\[
p_{i^\star 0}-\delta>0.5.
\]

- [ ] If no candidate clears the margin, use demonstration/GT rehearsal only;
  do not force a top-1 non-stock label.
- [ ] Save all candidate hashes, score components, margins, state mode, and
  teacher weights.
- [ ] Report teacher calibration separately on exact grounded states and
  extrapolated teacher states. This is a readout, not a launch gate.

With four exchangeable candidates, a random argmax is non-stock with
probability \(3/4\). Therefore non-stock fraction or "positive teacher
fraction" is not evidence of learned selection and is not a metric used to
justify this run.

For every WM-positive state, draw and save a hash-seeded
\(k_s\sim\operatorname{Uniform}\{1,2,3\}\) and set

\[
i_{\mathrm{random}}=(i^\star+k_s)\bmod 4.
\]

Thus random assignment is score-blind, never identical to the WM assignment,
and may select the reference candidate. The matched-random teacher must reuse
the exact:

- state mask and total teacher weight;
- candidate pools;
- optimizer updates;
- flow noise/times;
- demonstration and GT rehearsal.

It changes only this frozen candidate assignment. Candidate and assignment
hashes are written before either policy job starts.

### Grounded GT manifest

Build the grounded teacher independently of the learned model:

- [ ] Use `policy_candidates[0]` as the reference action at every executed
  source-train snapshot.
- [ ] Eligible alternatives are `policy_candidates[1:4]` and, separately,
  `u_support`. An alternative is positive only when its canonical-task
  outcome tuple beats the reference in the same direction in both paired
  continuation repeats beyond replay tolerance.
- [ ] Choose the lexicographically best eligible outcome; break an exact tie
  by saved action hash. Give the selected first-ten chunk one uniform GT
  weight. If no alternative qualifies, emit no GT target.
- [ ] Mark selections from `u_support` as `support_gt` with demonstration,
  staged, or oracle provenance. Report `policy_gt` and `support_gt` behavior-
  training slices separately; neither may enter the WM/random candidate pool.
- [ ] Freeze one GT state mask, target hash, and weight manifest and reuse it
  unchanged in every trained policy checkpoint.

## V6.5 — Post-train the π0.5 flow interface

Freeze the scoring world model. Train the first-ten flow loss on grounded or
model-generated chunks and retain the ordinary full-50 demonstration
flow-matching loss.

The first v0.6 policy matrix is:

| arm | policy state | grounded teacher | generated teacher |
|---|---|---|---|
| `stock` | none | none | none |
| `v06_gt_current` | reset/current \(z_t^\ell\) | observed positive branch | none |
| `v06_gt_recurrent` | recurrent \(z_t^\ell\) | same observed positive branch | none |
| `v06_recurrent_random` | recurrent \(z_t^\ell\) | same observed positive branch | matched random |
| `v06_recurrent_wm` | recurrent \(z_t^\ell\) | same observed positive branch | v0.6 WM |

`v06_gt_current` and `v06_gt_recurrent` are two deployment modes of the same
trained `v06_gt` checkpoint, not independently optimized adapters.
`gt_current` versus stock tests whether the collected executed targets can
improve the deployed policy. `gt_recurrent` versus `gt_current` is therefore
a same-weight test of whether carried state helps under the same valid
targets; the training contract below also exposes this checkpoint equally to
both state modes so reset deployment is not out of distribution.
`recurrent_wm` versus `recurrent_random` tests whether the world model creates
useful additional supervision.

- [ ] Start all three trained checkpoints—GT-only, matched-random, and
  WM-generated—from the identical stock checkpoint and zero
  \(W_z\).
- [ ] On every grounded-GT target batch in all three checkpoints, run both
  reset/current and recurrent state forwards with the same target, flow
  noise/time, and weight; average their losses equally. Evaluate the shared
  `v06_gt` checkpoint in both deployment modes. Generated WM/random loss uses
  recurrent state only.
- [ ] Freeze and hash π0.5 PrefixVLM, π0.5 action expert, \(E_a,T,U\), EMA
  modules, and every world-model head. The policy-stage trainable whitelist is
  exactly the zero-initialized \(W_z\) projection; the scoring and deployment
  state checkpoints are byte-identical.
- [ ] Every optimization step in all three checkpoints contains the identical
  normalized base batch
  `{GT branch + demo rehearsal + stock-flow trust region}`. WM and random add
  their matched generated loss; GT-only adds a literal zero generated loss.
  Do not replace that zero with extra rehearsal.
- [ ] Match optimizer, update count, batch composition, loss normalizers, and
  base-loss coefficients across all three checkpoints.
- [ ] Use only source-train grounded branches. Label any demonstration,
  staged, or oracle-supported target as a training control in its manifest.
- [ ] Credit only the first ten actions of generated/branch chunks; keep the
  complete 50-action demonstration loss.
- [ ] Define the common trust loss as squared velocity deviation from frozen

### V6.1 design bindings (registered before any training)

State and modules (`lcwm/v06_model.py`):
- z_t^ℓ: 4 tokens × d_z=384 (token budget matched to v0.4/v0.5; no sweep).
- h_t^ℓ = the full π0.5 prefix hidden sequence (visual+language tokens of
  the real prompt) + pad mask — ONE feature stream, no early/late split.
- E_a: MLP over flattened (10 × (7 actions + 1 mask)) → 1 action token
  (matches prior ActionTransition convention).
- T_θ(z, E_a(u)): 2-layer pre-LN transformer encoder over [z ‖ a-token],
  returns updated z tokens.
- Anchor A(h): 4 learned queries cross-attend into h (2 blocks) → c_t.
- U_θ(z̄, h) = LN(c_t + 0.25·tanh R(c_t, z̄)) — observation-anchored,
  fixed β=0.25, NO learned gate. R: cross-attention of c into z̄ tokens +
  MLP, output layer zero-initialized (at init the state is exactly the
  anchored current observation).
- z_∅: learned init tokens; a_∅ = zero action with zero mask. Reset mode
  z^reset = U(T(z_∅, a_∅), h_t) — identical to recurrent at episode start
  by construction.
- D_current(z): current automaton grounding ONLY (per-subgoal
  currently-valid bits, event-milestone bits, ordered-prefix scalar).
- D_next(z̃): every candidate-dependent output — Δq (9), Δobj (8×3),
  next currently-valid bits, next event-milestone bits, 0→1 / 1→0 flip
  bits, damage, reward (milestone delta), success-by-100,
  P_valid(100), Q_valid at h ∈ {10,30,60,100}, τ_next (censored 101,
  scaled /101), and the ranking scalar s_θ(z̃) as its own head. D_next
  consumes pooled z̃ only — no h, no z_t, no D_current features.
- Policy interface: b_t = η·tanh(W_z·Pool(z_t)), Pool = token mean,
  W_z: 384→1024 zero-init, η = 1.0 fixed (bias bounded to [−1,1]^1024;
  same AdaRMS injection path as v0.4/v0.5).
- EMA: decay 0.995 over {E_a, T, U(anchor+R), z_∅}; EMA posterior is the
  latent-closure target only; asserted unreachable from the candidate
  scorer path.

Mechanical assertion suite: `scripts/test_v06_mechanical.py` implements
the eight registered launch assertions. They certify wiring only.

### 2026-07-31 post-run implementation audit — correction to interpretation

The behavior records above remain factual. The claim that iteration 1
faithfully tested the complete v0.6 learning contract is superseded by this
audit. The original preregistration and execution log remain unchanged so the
discrepancy is inspectable rather than rewritten after the result.

#### 1. The stored continuations are not sibling-CRN comparisons

The V6.2 binding above registered a candidate-indexed seed and simultaneously
described it as sibling-identical. The executed `cont_seed` contains
`branch_index * 40`; different candidate branches therefore used different
π0.5 flow-noise streams. Ranking labels, the `16/2` train/dev calibration
pairs, and the two grounded GT targets all mix first-ten candidate effect with
candidate-specific continuation noise. The two-repeats-agree rule reduces but
does not remove this confound: `25/150` canonical branch records disagree
between repeats on at least one outcome component.

Consequently, the iteration-1 continuations and every preference, calibration
margin, GT target, and learned checkpoint derived from them are historical
artifacts, not clean supervision for the next run. Phase-A source histories
and physical first-ten branches remain reusable after hash audit; phase B and
everything downstream must be regenerated.

#### 2. Distinct-GoalSpec success used the canonical BDDL goal

`outcome_tuple` calls `terminal_success(env)`, which evaluates the original
environment `goal_state`, for canonical and distinct GoalSpecs alike. The
distinct prompt and automaton were changed, but their highest-priority
`success_by_100` label was not. GoalSpec-specific progress/value supervision
is therefore partially corrupted even though each continuation did run under
its own prompt.

Two lower-level label errors compound this mismatch. Goal automata were
advanced only after each ten-action chunk, so transient flips, milestone
times, and success did not have action-level resolution. In addition, the
canonical environment termination flag could end a distinct-GoalSpec rollout
before that GoalSpec reached its own success or horizon. Both are supervision-
contract bugs, not evidence about whether language-conditioned dynamics work.

#### 3. Registered next-semantic heads were silent

`D_next.valid_bits`, `event_bits`, `flips_01`, and `flips_10` were instantiated
but never consumed by `train_v06_wm.py`. A checkpoint-to-seed-0 comparison
finds every parameter in those four heads bitwise unchanged. The claims that
all loss families were active refer only to the trainer's twelve enumerated
loss keys; they did not certify that every preregistered candidate-semantic
output received a target or gradient.

The distinct-goal pass trained current grounding plus shared `d_obj`; the
branch pass trained `d_q/d_obj`; canonical sequences trained physical effects
plus reward. The intended immediate action × language next-valid/event/flip/
reward matrix did not enter optimization in full.

#### 4. The support failure is about information content, not generic effects

The iteration-1 routing says “too few effect-resolved” rows, but `57/74`
audited groups already passed that flag. The problem is that object replay
tolerance froze to zero, so the chronologically first candidate group with
any nonzero object displacement could qualify.

For the 23 accepted train groups, median maximum candidate-pair object
dispersion is `3.85 mm`, only `7/23` exceed `5 mm`, the minimum is
`0.137 mm`, and only `1/23` has an immediate valid-bit disagreement. On held-
out groups the centered object-effect predictor has `0.475 mm` RMSE versus a
`0.462 mm` zero-effect baseline and approximately zero correlation. The
correct diagnosis is too little **task-relevant, outcome-separating,
reference-improving counterfactual support**, not too little numerical
physical motion.

#### 5. The policy schedule confounds the grounded-GT interpretation

The two GT rows were each cycled approximately 150 times in 300 updates. The
sorted rehearsal bank contained 878 rows, but only its first 300 entries were
consumed, covering t1/t2 and omitting t3–t5. The learned bias has substantial
total amplitude but very little across-state variation. The large saved action
shift therefore establishes only that `W_z` can change π0.5; it does not
establish useful state-conditioned coupling or isolate target quality as the
only policy-stage failure.

#### Corrected result scope and routing

- The recurrent LC state, transitioned candidate path, no-bypass decoder,
  recurrent teacher unroll, Wz-only interface, and public-LoHo evaluator are
  mechanically present.
- The model-generated policy channel was void because `0/208` teachers made
  WM/random/GT recurrent weights identical.
- The two-target grounded result is not a clean target-quality experiment
  because its labels were not sibling-CRN and its policy schedule was not
  task-balanced.
- No history-contrast rows were collected, so current-versus-recurrent
  behavior does not identify useful memory.
- Iteration 1 neither supports nor refutes the LCWM hypothesis.

The next mainline is the data/supervision-contract repair in
`2026-07-31.md`, followed immediately by a complete
WM → teacher → π0.5 post-training → public-LoHo rerun. Generic source scaling,
PEFT, and architecture sweeps are not the first action.
